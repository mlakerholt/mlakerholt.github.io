Gas delivery

Inlet O₂ is the oxygen fraction of the gas entering the vessel: 21% is air and 100% is pure oxygen. Fixed mode holds this composition; adaptive mode starts at 21% and enriches it through the DO cascade. This is separate from dissolved oxygen (% air saturation).

DO controller tuning

PI control retains the output needed to hold the setpoint. It increases agitation, then airflow; optional adaptive mode adds oxygen enrichment as a final stage. Fixed mode holds the selected inlet gas composition. Defaults are engineering assumptions, not vessel-specific calibration.

Actual modeled settings and delivered additions throughout the run. Dashed lines show configured limits. Feed pump speed is shown as liquid flow, not motor RPM. Core growth model

μ = μmax · S/(KS+S) · O₂/(KO₂+O₂) · f(pH,T)

Substrate and oxygen jointly limit growth, maintenance and product. The adapted product law is qP = αμ + βfSfO, with substrate allocation and a respiratory-capacity limit.
